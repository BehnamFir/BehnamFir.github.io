Profile

One research identity, developed across several engineering domains.

My research is unified by a central question: how can mathematical modelling, computation, and data-driven methods reveal—and improve—the dynamic behaviour of complex mechanical systems?

My current work focuses on vibration and structural-wave propagation in curved and thin-walled structures. I study how curvature, periodicity, local resonance, imperfections, prestress, and boundary conditions alter modal behaviour, attenuation, energy transport, and model validity.

Earlier work established a broader foundation in nonlinear MEMS, electrostatic instability, piezoelectric energy harvesting, structural damage detection, rotating machinery, mechanical design optimization, robotics, and deep-learning forecasting. This website presents these areas as a connected research portfolio rather than a page devoted to a single project.
